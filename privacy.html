Last updated: July 22, 2026

Royal Bake ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website (the "Site") and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message when you write to us.
  • Orders: your name, phone number, order details and any notes when you start an order or ask us to prepare something.

When you choose to pay by card, your payment is processed securely by our ordering provider, Toast — we do not receive or store your full card number. You can also choose to pay when you pick up your order.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and questions.
  • To prepare, confirm and fulfil your orders.
  • To improve our service and communicate with you about your order.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How Your Information Is Shared

Messages and orders sent through the Site are delivered to us by email (for example, through your own email app). If you choose to order through a third-party delivery service, that service collects and handles your information under its own privacy policy.

Cookies & Analytics

The Site keeps a small amount of information in your browser (such as the items in your order) so the Site works properly. We may use basic analytics to understand how the Site is used. You can clear this data through your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

We keep order and contact information only as long as needed to serve you and to meet any legal or business requirements, and then remove it.

Your Choices

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted on this page.
