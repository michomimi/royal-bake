ABOUT-PAGE VIDEO SLIDESHOW
==========================
Drop your video clips in THIS folder, named exactly:

  about-1.mp4
  about-2.mp4
  about-3.mp4   (and so on)

They autoplay one after another as a looping, cross-fading slideshow in
the "Who We Are" frame on the About page.

Notes:
- Use .mp4 (H.264) so every browser can play it.
- Videos play MUTED (browsers require muting to autoplay).
- Keep each clip small (ideally a few MB) so the page stays fast.
- Have more or fewer than 3? Tell me the count and I'll match the slots
  in about.html (or just name them about-1.mp4, about-2.mp4, ...).
