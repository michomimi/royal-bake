ROYAL BAKE — IMAGES FOLDER
==========================

Your logo is already here (logo.png) and wired into the header, footer
and browser tab. You don't need to touch it.

To add your photos later, drop them into images/menu/ using these exact
file names and they'll appear automatically (until then, you'll see a
labeled gold placeholder in the correct shape):

  images/menu/hero.jpg            → main photo on the home hero (portrait)
  images/menu/about.jpg           → photo in the About section (portrait)
  images/menu/chef-abu-abdo.jpg   → Chef Abu-Abdo portrait
  images/menu/chef-abdul.jpg      → Chef Abdul portrait

TIPS
----
• Portrait / vertical photos (roughly 4:5 or 3:4) fit the arched frames best.
• Keep files reasonably small (under ~500 KB each) so the site loads fast.
  Save as JPG at ~1200px on the long edge.
• Names are case-sensitive: use lowercase, exactly as shown above.
• Want per-dish photos on the menu too? Tell me and I'll switch the menu
  cards to an image layout and give you the naming list.
